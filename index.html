About this model

Board tops are the real photographs. Parts are extruded from footprints measured in the photos. Synthetic or assumed: part heights and plastic and metal colours; the PeriBoard stack height; PCB thickness; detail geometry such as connector contacts, IC leads, latch hooks and solder balls, and the micro-geometry that makes parts read as moulded, folded or soldered (the small rounded edges on housings, shells and IC bodies, the solder fillets at every lead and header foot, kept inside each lead's photographed outline, the pointed header-pin tips and the capacitor's bevelled rim: the photos show such features but none of their sizes, so typical values are used); the exploded view and the insertion motion (distances, the 28 degree angle and the latch travel); a cosmetic texture patch that is shown only while the latches are open; the surface finish (glossy solder mask, matte silkscreen, metallic pads and gold, matte IC markings), which is inferred from each pixel's colour in the photographs, not measured, with a typical reflectance assumed for the metal and a slight synthetic surface relief (silkscreen proud, pads below the mask); and the lighting: a synthetic studio (a large soft warm key, cool fill and rim, and a soft panel behind the kit that the glossy mask mirrors as a sheen), shown by default on a dark satin table with a graded dark backdrop and, with the Backdrop button, on a white sweep like the photographs (neither is the photographer's set-up; the sweep is drawn a little below paper white so the shadows stay visible), screen-space ambient occlusion, and a slight depth of field in the Hero view that is off while anything moves; the board colour was matched to the 3/4 photograph at two plain solder-mask patches by luminance only (within 10 percent; hue and highlights are approximate). Board sizes are photo-derived from standard-pitch features on each board (base about 160 by 87 mm, PeriBoard about 45 by 87 mm, Mercury about 35.5 by 69.6 mm, each within a few percent). The undersides of the base board and PeriBoard were not photographed and are shown plain. Mercury's back is photographed.

  • photo-verified: the printed designator or marking is legible in a photo (shape is photo-derived, height assumed)
  • photo-derived: footprint measured from a photo
  • assumed: not visible in any photo
